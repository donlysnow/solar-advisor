How It Works

What happens behind the scenes when you click "Get Recommendations."

1
Weather in. For Live Mode, we fetch real hourly weather (temperature, humidity, sunlight intensity) for your coordinates. For Historical Mode, we use a sample recorded dataset instead.
2
Solar forecast. A trained model combines that weather with your panel size to predict how much power your panels will generate, in 15-minute steps through the day.
3
Background load forecast. A second model predicts your household's baseline power draw (things always pulling power, plus estimated heating/cooling) from the same weather and time-of-day signals.
4
Surplus. We subtract the background load from the solar forecast. Positive surplus means your panels are producing more than the house is quietly using.
5
Scheduling. For each appliance in your list, we scan the day for the window with the most solar surplus, sized to how long that appliance runs.

Honest limitations

The background load model is trained on a sample dataset, not your actual home, so treat it as a reasonable estimate rather than a guarantee. It only knows what you tell it — your panel size and appliance list are self-reported, and each forecast is a one-time snapshot, not continuous monitoring.
